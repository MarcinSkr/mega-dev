#4471 · two date problems merged together

owner support deskknownreviewed 2026-10-29

Since April, people have told us their bookings land on the wrong day: a month flipped. By 4 October the desk had 412 unique reports in one merged ticket. The export contains 427 rows because 15 report IDs occur twice where its pages overlap.

Merged as one issue, but it contains two causes. The first is a parser bug: it reads dates month first while day-first tenants write the day first. The second is a form bug: booking-v3 shows a month-first hint to some day-first tenants. The fixes are tracked separately in issue #1 and issue #2.

Known. Two causes, two fixes queued.

The export count deduplicates report IDs before grouping. The cause depends on the tenant locale, booking form, and channel; the intake tag is only an initial guess.

What to do next

  • Fix the shared parser for the 379 reports in the parser group.
  • Fix the booking-v3 date hint and parsing for the 33 reports in the hint group.
  • Keep the groups separate in follow-up work: the form bug is not evidence that the shared parser is wrong for every report.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can contain more than one cause. Group by tenant configuration and channel before assigning work.
  • A paginated export can repeat IDs. Count unique IDs, not rows.
  • Write the page while the queue is still in your head.
